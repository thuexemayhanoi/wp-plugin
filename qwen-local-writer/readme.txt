Experimental, no-paid-API Qwen browser text generation for WordPress drafts.
No commercial AI provider account required; model and JS library must be downloaded on demand.
Requires compatible WebGPU computer and open browser tab. Do not deploy on production without staging tests and vendoring the external JS module.
Human factual review is mandatory. Technical QA score 70 is not a content quality or ranking guarantee.
